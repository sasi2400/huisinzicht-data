# HuisInZicht – Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

HuisInZicht is a free Chrome extension that shows public data about a home you are viewing on
funda.nl, pararius.nl / pararius.com or jaap.nl.

## What the extension does with data
- **On listing pages only**, it reads the address, asking price, living area, house type, energy label and
  listing date shown on the page, inside your own browser.
- It sends **only that address or its map coordinates** to public Dutch government and open-data services to look up
  information about that address: PDOK / Kadaster (address register, BAG, WOZ-waardeloket), CBS (statistics),
  Leefbaarometer (Ministry of BZK), RIVM Atlas Leefomgeving and the Klimaateffectatlas.
- It downloads a public file with regional market figures from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`).

## What it does not do
- No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no server of our own.
- It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
- It does not read other websites, your browsing history, or anything you type.

## Storage
Results are cached temporarily in your browser (`chrome.storage.session`), and the regional figures in
`chrome.storage.local`. Both stay on your device and are removed when you remove the extension.
